## About TourGo

TourGo connects travellers with trusted local tourism services across Sri Lanka.

Rather than relying only on fixed travel packages, TourGo gives travellers the freedom to discover services and build their journey according to their own preferences.

## What We Offer

- 🐘 **Safari Experiences** — Discover safari services and drivers for Yala, Udawalawe, Wilpattu, Minneriya, and more.
- 🏨 **Hotels** — Find and explore accommodation across Sri Lanka.
- 🚙 **Safari Drivers** — Discover driver profiles, vehicles, availability, and services.
- 🗺️ **Journey Planning** — Build a personalised Sri Lankan journey by selecting destinations and services.

## Our Technology

`React` · `Vite` · `Flutter` · `Spring Boot` · `PostgreSQL` · `Neon` · `GitHub Actions`

## Our Mission

> Make Sri Lankan travel easier for travellers while creating better digital opportunities for local tourism providers.
